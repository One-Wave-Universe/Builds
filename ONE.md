## What it is
Hex sandwich. Three differentials star to CENTER. Figure-8 (two square ferrites) *is* that CENTER — views up, actions down, 4×4 at the cross. Heavy-metal / copper strip under the 8 and under the hex bus lattice writes. Hex edges wear a magnetic skin (Permalloy / ferrite) so the lattice holds Br. V_BUS is fed by all leftover current and feeds everyone. Pack only on ask. Two-of-three current-sum (1.5 / 1.2, GAP wait) permits PUSH/FLIP on heading windings. Those windings *are* the actuator. Hysteresis is the settled whole loop. Minor loop = cheap habit.

## Nodes
V_TOP · CENTER · V_BUS  — never one pour.

## Seats
Edge centers, clockwise A+ B+ C+ A- B- C-.
