# V_BUS and lactate

Analogy for the proposal. Not biochemistry in the cell.

School-poster lactate = poison that makes you tired. That map is wrong, and it would make the bus wrong too.

Lactate is mostly: keep working when the fast path is hot, move energy to wherever can use it, and *also* tickle sensors that say ease off. The burn people feel is that signal mixed with H+ and other metabolites, not lactate alone.

---

## Three jobs, three bus jobs

| Lactate (body) | V_BUS (cell) |
| --- | --- |
| Shuttle — leftover fast energy goes to other tissue that can burn it | Reinjection — collapse goes to the rail, any cell may draw |
| Buffer / keep the fast path legal (NAD, not a dead-end dump) | Return instead of heat-only flyback |
| Signal — afferents + receptors: perceived exertion, “ease off” | Ask pack; if still falling, **send up** (redline) |

Waste-only thinking → dump collapse as heat, no rail, no flag. That is the old lactate story. Rejected.

---

## Debt vs poison

High lactate means production outran clearance. High-and-falling bus means return + pack ask outran spend.

Clearance in the body: other muscle, heart, liver. Clearance here: other cells on the rail + pack refill.

When clearance works, high flux is *performance*, not failure. A busy bus that stays in band is a working loop, not strain.

Strain is **level still dropping while ask is already on**. That is the burn-shaped flag. Not “bus voltage exists.”

---

## What not to copy

- Do not store “lactate” as a software variable.
- Do not make V_BUS into CENTER.
- Do not say the lattice feels burn. Say the field receives up and PASSes.
- Do not treat send-up as the only story. The shuttle (return to rail) is the main lactate-like job. The flag is the afferent on top.
