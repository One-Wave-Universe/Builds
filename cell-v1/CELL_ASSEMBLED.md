# Cell — assembled

One-page CELL_V1 integration authority beneath `CELL.md`.

**Status:** topology is locked as a proposal. `LOG.md` is still the hardware evidence gate.

## Geometry

- Point-up hexagon.
- Six edge connections: A+ → B+ → C+ → A− → B− → C−.
- Three mirrored axes: A+↔A−, B+↔B−, C+↔C−.
- Six seats are three differential channels, not six independent gates.

## Nucleus / brain side

One **square figure-8 toroidal nucleus** sits on the lattice-bus / vagus-nerve side of the cell.

Its job is retained-state bias and participation in the next physical decision. It is not CENTER and it is not V_BUS.

## Three mirrored gate pairs

The three axes resolve one ternary lean:

- DOWN = negative lean
- HOLD = centered live state
- UP = positive lean

CHOICE, PIVOT, and FLIP are coupled gate functions. The transistor implementation remains an engineering task; the topology does not.

No global clock. Threshold crossings, hysteresis, retained state, bus condition, and neighboring consequences determine transitions.

## Outer field shell

Two **round figure-8 toroidal structures** form one combined outer electrical / magnetic field.

They carry **six windings total**, organized as **two mirrored groups of three**.

The outer 3+3 shell expresses the resolved − / (0) / + lean as the larger differential bias used for motor control.

## CENTER and V_BUS

- **CENTER:** local lean reference.
- **V_BUS:** shared energy / readiness / reinjection rail.
- CENTER ≠ V_BUS.
- Collapse energy returns to V_BUS, never to CENTER.

## Unified loop

```
sensor / neighbor state
        ↓
square figure-8 nucleus
        ↓
three mirrored gate pairs
        ↓
− / (0) / + ternary lean
        ↓
two outer round figure-8s
mirrored 3 + 3 windings
        ↓
motor / field action
        ↓
inductive return
        ↓
V_BUS / lattice consequence
        ↓
next nucleus / gate state
```

No branch is allowed to become an independent controller.

## Flower / scale

Seven cells may tile into a flower through mirrored edge contacts. Shared lattice behavior is a scale-up hypothesis until the single-cell loop is measured.

The outer shell of neighboring cells may couple into a larger field structure; that coupling strength and usefulness are not yet evidence.

## Phase-I bench gate

Before scale-up, demonstrate:

1. stable local CENTER under switching;
2. repeatable ternary differential response;
3. state-dependent magnetic response after different prior writes;
4. inductive return to V_BUS with energy accounting;
5. one closed loop in which the returned consequence changes the next physical decision.

If identical probes after different prior writes do not produce distinguishable responses, stop and revise the cell.

## Locked topology

- square figure-8 = nucleus / brain side;
- three mirrored gate pairs;
- two outer round figure-8s;
- six outer windings = mirrored 3+3;
- − / (0) / + ternary lean;
- event-driven / no global clock;
- CENTER separate from V_BUS;
- reinjection re-enters the same loop.

## Still engineering / measurement

Magnetic material, turns, coupling coefficient, gate transistor map, write depth, fade law, bus impedance, field strength, torque, retention time, recovery fraction, thermal behavior, and scale-up performance.

Do not convert those unknowns into claims.
