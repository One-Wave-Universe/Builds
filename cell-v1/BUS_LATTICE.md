# Bus-side lattice

V_BUS is the shared circulation / readiness / reinjection rail of the lattice.

It is **not** the square figure-8 nucleus and it is not CENTER.

## Per-cell relationship

Each cell contributes:

- one square figure-8 nucleus on the brain/lattice-bus/vagus side;
- three mirrored differential gate pairs;
- two outer round figure-8 field structures;
- six outer windings as mirrored 3+3;
- a local CENTER;
- access to shared V_BUS.

Outer-winding collapse is steered back to V_BUS. Bus condition and neighbor consequences may then affect the next nucleus/gate decision.

## Lattice state

The lattice state is therefore not a single magnetic loop living on the bus. It is the coupled result of many cells' local retained state, gate leans, outer fields, returned energy, and neighbor interactions.

Whether that coupling produces useful collective behavior is a Phase-II measurement question.
