# Stack

Top to bottom, one hex:

```
V_TOP
three diffs                         ← ternary leans
round figure-8 toroid               ← differential side (round, not square)
CENTER                              ← lean home. Not a core. Not the bus.
square figure-8                     ← lattice + bus side
V_BUS                               ← leftover current. Feeds every lean.
PACK                                ← pays loss only
```

The round toroid is the ternary. The square figure-8 is the lattice and the bus. Same collapse current writes the square on the way back to the bus.

No comparators anywhere in this stack.

Heavy metal under the square can be the copper itself until it is plated. Do not wait on a fab to keep the two shapes apart.
