## Gray domain-wall catalog (not CELL identity)

Permalloy film: in-plane, low Hc (~15 Oe class in unpatterned film; geometry changes this). Wall energy and pinning rise as constrictions narrow (tens to hundreds of Oe). Pinning sites: notches, edge roughness, crystallinity, composition steps. Walker / precessional / turbulent motion regimes exist in stripes. Stochastic pinning is real at intermediate fields.

CELL does **not** import racetrack registers, current-driven bit shift, or an STT chip. The sheet is a **groove**: practice moves/pins walls along shared edges; remanence holds them.

Useful Gray facts for the bench:
- Pin on purpose (edge geometry / shared-side constriction) or the wall smears.
- Too-soft film + no pin = global blob (fail).
- Too-hard film = first habit freezes (fail Test I).
- Stochastic pinning at some drive levels means repeat the two-lean probe; one shot is not a receipt.

## Analogue write law (whole flower)

1. Cell leaves HOLD. Nucleus fires. Local Br set.
2. Coupling into the sheet under that cell / shared edge moves or pins a wall.

5. Practice deepens. Opposite practice or fade must move it.

No processor in the write path. No copy of six sensor states in software.
