# Two choices, three moves

The algorithm in analog has two choices and three moves. Not seven choices. Not a clock.

## Two choices — FIELD and VOID

FIELD and VOID are the two sides. The choice is which side the event stands on.

```text
FIELD  = one body side, one plain round toroid, TA_F TB_F TC_F
VOID   = the opposed side, one plain round toroid, TA_V TB_V TC_V
```

Mirrored DC is the same stand, flipped. Choice is that flip. Not a software branch. The pair is already on one side or the other, or live in the middle while it has not committed.

HOLD is not a third choice. HOLD is not chosen yet. Tail on. No net side.

## Three moves — A, B, C

The moves are the three differentials. They are the lean.

```text
A = DA = A+ − A−
B = DB = B+ − B−
C = DC = C+ − C−
```

A move is which axis carries the event, and which way that axis leans. Six seats, three numbers. Axes, not voltage levels.

The seven bands are how hard a move expresses or compresses. They are not extra moves. ±1 / ±2 / ±3 is strength, not a new letter.

## Do not mash

- Choice is FIELD or VOID. Not A/B/C.
- Move is A, B, or C. Not the seven-band scale.
- POINT / PATH / FIELD at larger scale is not an alias for A/B/C.
- CHOICE / PIVOT / FLIP names, where used, do not replace the two sides or the three axes.
- Lattice DC is the reinjection loop. It is not a fourth move.
- Helmholtz weather is not a choice.

## One event

1. Live middle. No side yet.
2. A move on A, B, or C. The differential walks.
3. Choice commits FIELD or VOID. Mirrored DC is the other side.
4. Collapse enters lattice DC. Shared hysteresis layer keeps the muscle. V_BUS keeps the energy.
5. Next event meets both: the side that was chosen, and the axis that was moved.

Two choices. Three moves. Same iron.
