# DC / AC / RC

They did not get thrown out. They go **up**, as field and void.

Not three power supplies. Three kinds of current in the same path. All analog. No comparator sorts them.

| Kind | What it is | Which way is up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DC** | Committed flow. Axis left the wobble. Net current one way. The lean as a stand. | **Void.** |
| **AC** | Drive across the dead zone. The excursion that tries to leave HOME. The turn. | **Field rotation.** |
| **Mirrored DC** | The same stand, flipped. AC rides with it. | The mirror of void, still up. |
| **RC** | What remains after the event. Not a third rail. | Goes up with them. |

---

## Where they sit

- The **round figure-8 toroid** is the differential / ternary side. DC, AC, and the mirrored DC live there.
- Collapse still goes through the **square figure-8** onto **V_BUS**. That current is the write. It is not RC.
- RC is what the round toroid still holds after the event. If you call V_BUS "RC" you mashed dump and hold again.

---

## One event

1. HOLD (no net DC, tail on).
2. AC drive. Field rotates. D walks.
3. Two-of-three: DC commits (void) or you fall back to HOLD. The mirror is the other way.
4. Collapse through the square into V_BUS. That is the write.
5. RC left on the round toroid.

Next event sees RC as the new home offset. Use deepens. Disuse fades.
