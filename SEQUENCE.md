# Build sequence

Same CENTER the whole way. Do not skip a step and name the next one done.

## 0. Bench
400-point board, 9 V = V_TOP, CENTER as its own row (not blue), two 2N7000, 10k/100k, magamp toroid on CENTER, DMM.

## 1. See D1 (BC-DC)
One pair. `D = DB − DC` vs CENTER. HOLD wobble vs lean. Leftover Br on the toroid after you let go.

## 2. Gate leftover current
Schottky + 10 nF + 1 µF BUS row. Collapse does not hit CENTER. BUS ≠ CENTER.

## 3. D2 (TC-AC) same CENTER
Drive the pair out and back. Zero-cross. Phase. Same V0.

## 4. Second axis drinks the rail
Live BUS vs dead BUS. Different answer = reinject is real.

## 5. D3 / third seat
Third pair on the same CENTER. Electrical rotation A→B→C. Still a slice, not a volume.

## 6. Bidirectional gates
Back-to-back FETs on an axis. PUSH one way, FLIP the other, PASS to BUS. Never both on.

## 7. Two-of-three + PERMIT
Current-sum, 1.5 / 1.2, GAP wait. Heading windings only.

## 8. Figure-8 + lattice skin
Two squares on CENTER. Magnetic skin on hex edges. Views = last actions + sent-up. Repeat.

## 9. Flower slice
Seven hexes, shared BUS edges. Hex is still a slice.
