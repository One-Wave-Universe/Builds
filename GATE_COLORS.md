- **Brown** — bare dirt. Barely a thought. Thought experiment.
- **Green** — green earth. Ready for growth.
- **Yellow** — yellow brick road. Exploratory math. Do not get too excited.
- **Bronze** — bronze bust. Validated once.
- **Silver** — silver statue. Second independent application.
- **Gold** — golden road. Greater proof across applications.
- **Gray** — Standard Model view on concrete. Looks done. Prevents SM drift. Prevents fake growth by plastering SM over the garden.
- **Red** — brick through the glass house of SM assumptions. Only from Gray, only if a real experiment E(x,r) forced the negation.

Do not mark a build Gold because the schematic is pretty.
